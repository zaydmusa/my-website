I'm a third-year at UC Davis studying Economics and Middle East/South Asia Studies, with minors in Computer Science and Psychology. On paper those don't have much in common. In practice they're all ways of asking why people do what they do, and what would make it easier for them.

That question is what pulls me toward product and analytics work. Last summer at Capital One I rebuilt a sales commission structure, which was mostly a matter of finding where the rules paid people to do the wrong thing. Before that I worked on product at a medtech startup, on finance at a law firm, and on operations at the Governor's Office.

On campus I spend most of my time helping other students get good at this: running Product Space, teaching at Davis Consulting Group, and helping lead the Bengali Student Association.
