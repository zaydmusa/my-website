Zayd Musa.

Selected works, 2024 – 2026 · mixed media

I paint on weekends. During the week I work in other media: SQL, spreadsheets, Figma, and rooms full of people who need to agree on something.

I study Economics and Middle East/South Asia Studies at UC Davis, with minors in Computer Science and Psychology. That's four ways of asking the same question: why do people do what they do, and what would make it easier?

Everything on these walls started as a blank page and somebody's problem.

— Z.M.
